# GovAssist 🇮🇳

## Know Before You Go.

GovAssist is a web-based government service preparation assistant designed to help citizens understand what they need before visiting a government office.

It helps users explore government services, understand eligibility, follow application procedures, prepare required documents, check their readiness, identify the relevant office, and track their application journey.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Citizens often visit government offices without knowing:

- What documents are required
- Whether they are eligible
- What procedure they need to follow
- Which office or department they should visit
- Whether they are actually ready to apply

This can lead to:

**Unnecessary travel → Missing documents → Wasted time → Repeat visits**

The problem can be especially difficult for elderly citizens, rural citizens, first-time applicants, and people with limited digital knowledge.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

GovAssist provides a simple step-by-step preparation journey before a citizen visits a government office.

### User Journey

```text
Select Government Service
          ↓
Understand Eligibility
          ↓
View Application Procedure
          ↓
Check Required Documents
          ↓
Answer Preparation Questions
          ↓
Check Readiness
          ↓
Find Relevant Office
          ↓
Track Application
```
## ✨ Features

## 🔎 Government Service Search

Users can search and explore different government services through a simple interface.

## ✅ Eligibility Guidance

Users can understand the basic eligibility requirements for a selected government service.

## 📋 Document Checklist

GovAssist provides a checklist to help users prepare the required documents before visiting an office.

## 📝 Step-by-Step Procedure

Government procedures are presented in simple steps so citizens can understand what they need to do.

## 🎯 Readiness Assessment

GovAssist checks the user's preparation and provides a readiness score before visiting the office.

## 🏢 Office Guidance

Users can view relevant office information for the selected government service.

## 📊 Application Tracking

Users can track the progress of their application through the GovAssist tracking system.

## 🤖 GovAssist AI Assistant

The integrated AI assistant provides guidance related to government services, documents, eligibility, and procedures.
